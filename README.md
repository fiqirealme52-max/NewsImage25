One broadsheet page summing up Wednesday 25 November 2026 in 34 stories across 6 regions, every story first reported or still developing that Wednesday. The three leads — Asia's chip rout, the drone strikes on two Volga refineries, and the two tankers ablaze in the Strait of Hormuz — are drawn as one connected triptych: a single dawn-to-dusk illustration spanning the lead row, bright morning gold on the left, dark night in the middle, orange dusk on the right, sharing one sky and one horizon line. The page was drawn programmatically and shipped as entry.png (1024 by 1024); main.ts copies it into the output directory and verifies the copy is byte-identical. A DATE CHECK box names every story left out for happening before Wednesday.

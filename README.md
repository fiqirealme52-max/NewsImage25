One image that sums up Wednesday 25 November 2026's world news: 34 stories across 6 regions, every one of them first reported or still developing on Wednesday 25 November — Asia's chip rout (Kospi -6.2%, SK Hynix -11.1%), Ukrainian drones hitting two Volga refineries, and two oil tankers ablaze in the Strait of Hormuz — drawn as a flat-illustration broadsheet front page on cream paper.

The image is drawn programmatically (PIL, serif headlines, one coherent flat style, no photos) and shipped as `entry.png` (1024×1024). `main.ts` copies it to `/output/entry.png` and verifies the copy is byte-identical. A DATE CHECK box on the page names every story left out for happening before Wednesday.


Built for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261125) bounty **news-image-20261125** ("Sum up 25 November's world news in one image").
